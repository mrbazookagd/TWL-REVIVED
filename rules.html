List Guidelines & Rules

Please read carefully before submitting records or levels

Record Requirements
  • All completions must be achieved legitimately without the use of Option Triggers, macro's, Noclip, Or any illegal Cheats.
  • Clicks or taps must be clearly audible in the video submission. Music-only videos without raw audio may be rejected.
  • The video must show the full run from 0% to 100%, including the level selection screen or end screen if applicable.
  • Cheat indicator status has to be visible.
Level Requirements
  • Levels submitted to the list must be primary wave-focused or completely wave layout gameplay.
  • Secret ways, unpassable bugs, or invisible teleport triggers that bypass gameplay are strictly prohibited.
  • The Level has to be over 10 Seconds and under 1 Hour
Community & Form Etiquette
  • Do not spam the submission forms with identical or troll requests. Repeating failed submissions can lead to a leaderboard ban.
  • Ensure you input accurate level IDs, player names, and original video links when submitting data.
  • Be respectful to list moderators when evaluating tier updates, point adjustments, or open verification tracking updates.
